# PicAlive AR

Point your phone camera at a picture, poster or billboard and watch it come alive.

**Open the app:** https://afaqali11.github.io/picalive/

## How to use it
1. Open the link above on your phone in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Tap **Add**, take or pick a photo of the picture you want to animate, and choose an animation.
3. Tap **Open AR camera**, allow the camera, and point at the real picture.

Everything runs on your phone. Your pictures are not uploaded anywhere.

## What is inside
`index.html` is the whole app in one file. It uses three.js for drawing and MindAR for image tracking, both bundled inside the file, so it needs no other downloads.
